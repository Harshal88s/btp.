How to fight

Controls

Every key can be changed in Settings, along with movement sensitivity, CPU difficulty and sound. In 1P mode both key sets control you. Double-tap left or right to dash. On a phone, turn it sideways: the arena fills the screen with the touch pad on top.

Combos. Hits that land while the opponent is reeling chain together. Damage scales down after the third hit, so finish with a special.
Powers. Every special does 12 damage and every super does 30, so the fight is about timing, not the character. While a power plays, the opponent is held in place and the camera focuses on the move.
Crouching. Hold down to duck under beams, shields, orbs and daggers. Punch while crouched for an uppercut that launches, kick for a sweep that trips.
Rounds. Best of three, 60 seconds each. When time runs out, the fighter with more health takes the round.
