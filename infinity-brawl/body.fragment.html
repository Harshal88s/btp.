The roster

Seven heroes. Three villains. Ten move sets.

Every fighter has a special on a short cooldown and a super that needs a full meter. Flyers get a second jump. Heavyweights hit harder and take more.

How to fight

Controls

In 1P vs CPU, both key sets control you, so arrows or WASD both work. In 2P mode, Player 1 takes the arrows and Z X C V, Player 2 takes WASD and J K L I. Click the arena once if keys seem ignored.

Combos. Hits that land while the opponent is reeling chain together. Damage scales down after the third hit, so finish with a special.
Meter. Dealing and taking damage fills the gold bar. At 100% press Super for a cinematic that cannot be blocked.
Blocking. Holding block cuts damage to a fifth and prevents stun. Thanos' snap and every other super still land.
Rounds. Best of three, 60 seconds each. When time runs out, the fighter with more health takes the round.

Your own HD art and movie sounds

Drop files in, no code changes

The game draws every character in code, but it will swap in your files automatically. Add an assets folder next to index.html.

HD character images

  1. Save one PNG per fighter with a transparent background, full body, facing right.
  2. Name them assets/sprites/ironman.png, cap.png, thor.png, hulk.png, spiderman.png, panther.png, strange.png, thanos.png, loki.png, venom.png.
  3. Reload. The image becomes the in-fight sprite, the select portrait and the roster card. Missing files fall back to the drawn version.

Voice lines and sound effects

  1. Put clips in assets/sounds/<id>/, for example assets/sounds/venom/.
  2. Name them by moment: intro (signature line at round start), special, super, hit (grunt), win. Use .mp3, .ogg, .wav or .m4a.
  3. For variety add hit2.mp3, hit3.mp3, special2.mp3: one is picked at random each time.
  4. Global effects go in assets/sounds/ as punch, kick, hit, block, ko, round.

Good to know

  1. Clips from films are copyrighted. Fine for you and your friends, not for a public site.
  2. Keep clips short (under 3 seconds) and normalise the volume so grunts are not louder than lines.
  3. Sound only starts after the first tap or key press. That is a browser rule, not a bug.

Host it free

Put it on the internet in five minutes

The whole game is three files: index.html, style.css, game.js. Any free static host will serve it.

GitHub Pages

  1. Create a public repository and upload the three files to its root.
  2. Open Settings → Pages, set Source to Deploy from a branch, pick main and / (root), save.
  3. Your game is live at https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/ within a minute or two.

Netlify Drop

  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop.
  2. Drag the folder with the three files onto the page.
  3. You get a something.netlify.app link instantly. Rename it in Site settings.

Vercel or Cloudflare Pages

  1. Push the folder to GitHub.
  2. Import the repo in Vercel or Cloudflare Pages. Framework: None. Build command: empty. Output directory: /.
  3. Deploy. A custom domain is optional and also free to attach.

About

Infinity Brawl is a small, free, browser-only fighting game in the spirit of the viral two-button brawlers. All art is drawn procedurally in code, so the page loads in a blink and works offline once opened. No account, no download, no ads, no tracking. Your win tally is stored only in this browser.
