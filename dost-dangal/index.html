How to fight

Controls

Keyboard for desktop, on-screen pad on phones and tablets. Two players share one keyboard.

Combos. Hits that land while the opponent is still reeling chain into a combo. Damage scales down after the third hit, so finish with a special.
Meter. Dealing and taking damage fills the gold bar. At 100% press Super for a cinematic that cannot be blocked.
Blocking. Holding block cuts damage to a fifth and prevents stun. Specials still push you back.
Rounds. Best of three, 60 seconds each. When time runs out the fighter with more health takes the round.

About

Dost Dangal is a small, free, browser-only fighting game in the spirit of the viral two-button brawlers. The twist is the cast: it is whoever you put in it. Everything runs on this page. No account, no download, no ads, no tracking. Your fighters and win tally live only in this browser unless you share the code.

Be kind. Only add friends who are in on the joke. Keep catchphrases something you would say to their face.

Open source. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Fork it, reskin it, add your own moves.
